## Boundary tasks and services

The optional tutorial exposes four persisted lessons: `move` (Move and steer), `map` (Find a safe hunt), `kill` (Earn your first crowns), `gear` (Earn and equip gear). All are fixed instructional objectives, still unbranched; skipping the tutorial is not a meaningful branch. Keep these in the remaining design inventory even though they award no quest reward.

Town next-goal prompts restate the settlement tasks rather than creating new quests. Slayer rank rewards are automatic milestones, not separate contracts. Shops, forging, class training, resident trades, home purchases, building upgrades, hiring, rest and gathering are services/progression actions, not authored quests. Council decrees, reparations and emergent sieges are a separate choice-bearing simulation, not evidence that the linear quest rows above branch. Future authored tasks added to those systems must enter this inventory.
