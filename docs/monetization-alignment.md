The Android HTML is generated by tools/restore-game-source.py. The root
index.html is a separate older cosmetic preview, not the packaged Android
shop. tools/align-monetization.py applies changes after the historical source
hash checks. Do not edit the archived split payload to make these changes.

No price is invented or submitted. Decide crown quantities/prices, pet prices,
and whether paid dragons retain the Ashvault unlock before enabling these drafts.
The current draft pet delivery grants an inactive pet at half player level, or
adds recovery protection to an already owned pet without duplicating/healing it.
It does not change the free quest reward. These character-bound consumables
are not account-wide pet licenses. Account-wide restoration would need a
separate durable entitlement design before sales are enabled.

Paid XP/levels are not approved for new sales. Recovery of a previously paid
receipt is retained to avoid taking away a legitimate customer purchase.
Previously consumed items cannot be reconstructed from Play alone after losing
the save. Existing local/cloud-save receipt ledgers prevent replay on the saved
character, but rollback to an older backup can replay locally retained receipts;
a server ledger is required for robust cross-device/rollback protection.

## Controlled test before enabling sales

1. Verify Console app package, product IDs, active buy options, countries/prices,
   highest uploaded versionCode and license-tester account. Do not confuse closed
   track membership with license testing. Draft products stay disabled until
   exact contents/prices are approved. Existing public builds can see activated
   products, so do not activate a legacy SKU merely for testing.
2. Back up a normal existing character and record crowns, pets, XP, level, gear,
   quest progress and other slots. Install an eligible candidate with the
   maintained identity/signing key without uninstalling or clearing storage.
3. On the license tester, confirm the checkout identifies a test purchase and
   select the test instrument that always approves. Never use a real payment
   method. Test cancellation, decline and pending approval/decline separately.
4. Check one grant, durable save before acknowledgement/consumption, restart,
   repeated restore and repeated callback. Another character must not receive
   consumables. Cosmetic restore should recover verified durable ownership.
5. Test lost connection after payment, full storage and retry, corrupted local
   receipt metadata, and pet defeat/recovery. Verify existing characters and
   all unrelated progress survive the candidate upgrade.
6. Record build SHA/version, product ID, tester/test-payment confirmation,
   before/after values, restoration and Play order acknowledgement. A simulation
   is not evidence for this gate. Publish no public release until reviewed.
